**Signing World** is a platform that celebrates the Deaf community, Deaf culture, amplifies Deaf voices, and advocates for inclusion and accessibility. This space educates, inspires, and connects Deaf individuals, families, allies, educators, and organizations.

Through stories, education, sign language, and lived experiences, Signing World challenges misconceptions about Deafness and highlights the strength, identity, and richness of Deaf culture, especially in Kenya and across Africa.

Here, Deaf voices are not silent.

They are seen.

They are powerful.

They are Signing World.

**_Explore our stories, learn, and join the movement._**
