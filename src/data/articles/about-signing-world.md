Signing World was born from a deep passion for Deaf culture, sign language, and social inclusion.

As a Kenya Sign Language (KSL) user, mentor, and advocate, I have witnessed both the beauty and the challenges within the Deaf community; from resilience and creativity to barriers in education, employment, and access to information.

This platform exists to:

- Celebrate Deaf identity and culture
- Educate the public about Deafness and sign language
- Share real stories from the Deaf community
- Advocate for inclusion, dignity, and accessibility
- Create a safe space for learning and dialogue

Signing World is more than a blog. It is a movement toward understanding, respect, and equality.

If you believe in inclusion, visibility, and change you belong here!
